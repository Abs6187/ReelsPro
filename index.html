Project Overview

ReelsPro is an innovative platform where users can share and watch short videos. It features an AI chatbot for user assistance and a content moderation system to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone.

Upload and share your favorite moments with the community.

Key Features

User-friendly Interface

Intuitive design that makes video sharing and viewing a breeze for users of all technical levels.

AI-Powered Chatbot

Get instant answers to your questions with our intelligent chatbot powered by Groq and Vercel AI SDK.

Content Moderation

Advanced AI moderation to ensure all content meets community guidelines and standards.

High Performance

Optimized with server-side rendering (SSR) and static site generation (SSG) for lightning-fast load times.
